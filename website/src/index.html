Portfolio

Where do the weights come from?

Multiply return on invested capital (ROIC) by free cash flow yield. Divide each positive score by the sum of the scores to find its target weight.

Score = ROIC × FCF yield

Alder Works: 0.10 × 0.06 = 0.006. Its share of the total score is 0.006 / 0.010 = 60%.

An asset with a negative input is excluded. A zero score receives zero weight. If no score is positive, the method returns no portfolio.
