Portfolio

Where do the weights come from?

Score = ROIC × FCF yield

Multiply return on invested capital by free cash flow yield. Divide each positive score by the sum of the scores to find its target weight.

Alder Works scores 0.10 × 0.06 = 0.006. The total score is 0.010, so its target weight is 60%.

Exclude an asset if either input is negative. A zero score receives zero weight. If no score is positive, the method returns no portfolio.

Ownership model under research

Ownership and authorisation.

Participants could follow the same method while retaining individually attributed holdings. Verifying who owns a holding remains an open research question. Each payment requires the owner’s authorisation.

Joining the VOLPAROSSA network does not open a financial account or commit money to an investment.

Local paper-payment test

One instruction.
One transfer.

Bank saves the owner’s signed instruction before submitting it. Core records the transfer and its receipt. After a restart, Bank verifies the saved instruction and reads the original result. Retrying the accepted instruction leaves both balances unchanged.
