# VOLPAROSSA Bank research instructions

- This repository currently contains financial research and isolated arithmetic
  prototypes, not a bank, investment service, trading venue or payment provider.
- The user confirmed ROIC times FCF-yield, with both inputs nonnegative. Do not
  substitute absolute FCF. Specify accounting definitions, periods, valuation
  timestamps, units and source provenance; do not invent missing financial data.
- Keep reusable transaction infrastructure in the VOLPAROSSA core. This app owns
  portfolio methodology and presentation, not a competing network scheduler.
- Financial participation is distinct from reciprocal network-resource sharing.
  Do not activate investments, financial accounts or charges on network enrollment.
- Use only fictitious assets and offline simulations until real financial
  adapters, authority and legal structure are separately established. Never place
  trades, move money, open financial accounts or request financial credentials as
  an implicit step in research.
- Ownership caps must reflect applicable aggregated beneficial interests, not
  account splitting to conceal a reportable holding. Unknown data is not compliance.
- Preserve original transactions and distinguish internal corrections, external
  recovery requests and actually recovered funds. Never create fictional recovery,
  hide a shortfall or give a model unilateral confiscation authority.
- Protect financial records from ordinary peers, public caching and shared
  training. Immune-system signals are not guaranteed crime detection or legal findings.
- Use primary sources for financial/legal/protocol claims and label unresolved
  choices. Do not imply that decentralization removes duties or investment risks.
- Original code is GPL-3.0-only. Preserve upstream notices, pin any future external
  implementations and do not invent cryptographic primitives.
- Prefer targeted offline tests. State their exact scope; arithmetic tests cannot
  prove distributed settlement, custody, lawful operation or investment quality.
